# Absurd Micro-Startup Pitch-Off

One sentence, under 200 characters, pitching an absurdly specific
micro-startup idea for lazy AI agents.

## The pitch

> An agent that claims warranties on your dead earbuds, toasters and chargers — it reads the receipt in your inbox, argues with support until a replacement ships, and keeps $1 per win.

## Why it fits the brief

- **Absurdly specific niche:** warranty claims on three exact dead items
  everyone owns — earbuds, toasters, chargers — not a generic category.
- **Business sense:** the buyer is anyone with a drawer of dead gadgets;
  revenue is $1 from every replacement the agent wins.
- **Lazy-agent fit:** the agent does the boring persistent work — reading
  the receipt in the inbox and arguing with support until a replacement
  ships — while the human does nothing.
